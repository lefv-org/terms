# Reputation

Reputation is a contribution score shown on reader profiles. It is not a professional qualification or a rating of legal advice.

Automatic reputation updates are currently unavailable. Do not expect upvotes or accepted source suggestions to change your displayed score.

The scoring rules cover eligible upvotes on comments, articles you wrote or suggested, and accepted source suggestions. Self-votes and some other upvotes are excluded.

Contribute to help other readers. Do not submit content only to increase a score.
