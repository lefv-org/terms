# Reputation

Reputation is a score related to your contributions. It reflects contributions that other readers found useful. It is not a professional qualification or a rating of legal advice.

Eligible upvotes on your comments, articles you wrote, or articles you suggested can increase your reputation. Acceptance of a source suggestion can also increase it. Not every upvote earns reputation, and voting for your own contribution does not earn it.

Contribute to help other readers. Do not submit content only to increase a score.
