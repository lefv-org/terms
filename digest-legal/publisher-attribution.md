# Publisher attribution and duplicates

## Source credit

The service uses the article's address to identify its publisher. A feed can include articles from another publication, so the feed owner's name alone does not determine the source shown on the card.

For National Law Review articles, the service can credit a law firm identified on the page. Otherwise it credits National Law Review. Articles from other supported publications are credited to those publications.

Source credit does not transfer ownership of an article. Open the publisher's page for the full article and its author details.

## Repeated articles

The service compares feed identifiers and article addresses to reduce repeated entries. It can recognize some different addresses for the same article, including known publisher address forms.

These checks compare identifiers and addresses, not the full article text. They reduce repeated entries but do not guarantee that every duplicate will be detected.

Use the article's report option if you find a problem with its source or details.
