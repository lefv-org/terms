# Content guidelines

Share reporting, analysis, and commentary of professional interest to lawyers. Suitable subjects include court decisions, regulatory changes, enforcement, transactions, and practice commentary from firms and journalists.

## What to leave out

- Confidential or privileged material. If you are not certain you may share it, do not share it.
- Marketing presented as analysis, or content intended only to increase a score.
- Defamatory claims or information that identifies someone when a court order prohibits it.
- Advice to an individual about their own matter. This is a reading feed, not a place to take instructions.

## Comments

Discuss the reasoning in an article and treat other readers with respect. Disagreement about a case can be useful; personal attacks are not.
