# Submission guidelines

## Suggest a link

1. Select **Suggest a link** in the sidebar.
2. Enter the web address of an article or publication. You can include `https://` or `http://`; if you leave out the scheme, the form uses `https://`.
3. Check whether the form identifies it as an article or a site. Use **It is an article** or **It is a site** to correct the choice.
4. Sign in to send the suggestion.

You can suggest a publication's home page or feed address. You do not need to find its RSS or Atom feed first.

## Choose useful content

An article should be legal reporting or analysis, available through a public link, and not already in the feed. A publicly reachable link can still lead to a publisher's paywall.

A publication is a better fit if it publishes regularly, writes for practitioners, and provides useful article details such as titles, summaries, and images. A suggestion does not guarantee that the publication or article will be added. The form reports rejected or duplicate article suggestions and asks you to wait if you reach a limit.

Follow the [Content guidelines](./content-guidelines.html). Use **New post** when you want to write your own contribution instead of suggesting someone else's link.
