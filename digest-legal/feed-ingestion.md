# How articles enter the feed

RSS and Atom are formats publishers use to list articles. The service reads selected publisher feeds, follows article links, and collects details for article cards.

These details include a title, summary, image, publication date when available, and a link to the publisher. The reading feed does not contain the full article body. Reading time is an estimate.

The service checks for repeated articles and identifies the publisher. It can attach subject tags when suitable information is available. Only approved public tags appear on article cards.

## Why an article can be absent

A publisher's feed can be empty or unavailable. A page may not provide the title, summary, image, or other details needed for a card. The service can also skip an article when it cannot identify a supported publisher.

Feed support does not guarantee that every article from a publication will appear. There is no promise about the time between publication and appearance in the feed.

These publisher feeds supply the service. To read digest.legal in your own reader, use [the public RSS feed](https://digest.legal/rss).
