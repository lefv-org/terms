# digest.legal documentation

[digest.legal](https://digest.legal/) collects legal news and analysis in one reading feed. Open the publisher's page to read the full article. Some publishers require a subscription.

## Find an article

Browse the feed to find article cards. Each card identifies the source and links to the article. Reading time is an estimate. Feed views such as Recent, Most upvoted, and Best discussions use different orders.

Use search to find articles about a subject. Open a tag or source page to browse articles with that tag or from that publication.

## Choose subjects

Sign in and open Filters to follow practice areas and jurisdictions, or block topics you do not want. Open My feed to use your selection.

You can propose a public tag from the Tags page. A moderator reviews new public tags before they appear in the public directory.

## Save and discuss

Sign in and select the bookmark control on an article. Open Bookmarks to find saved articles. Reading history lists articles you opened, and read marks help you identify them in the feed.

Open an article's discussion to read comments. Sign in to write a comment or add an upvote. Use an upvote to show that you found an article or comment useful.

## Subscribe with RSS

Add [the public RSS feed](https://digest.legal/rss) to your feed reader. It contains recent public articles with links back to digest.legal discussions and the publishers' pages. It is a public feed, not a copy of your personal feed.
