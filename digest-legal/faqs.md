# Frequently asked questions

## What is digest.legal?

A reading feed of legal news and commentary from different publications. It helps you find articles without visiting each publication separately. It does not give legal advice.

## Where do articles come from?

Articles come from publishers, including through their RSS and Atom feeds. The service shows article details and links to the original. Read [How articles enter the feed](./feed-ingestion.html) and [Publisher attribution and duplicates](./publisher-attribution.html) for more detail.

## Do I need an account?

You can browse the public feed, search, tags, and article discussions without an account. Sign in to save articles, keep reading history and read marks, vote, or write comments.

## Does the service contain the full article?

The publisher provides the full article. A link does not give you access to a publisher's paid content. Follow the publisher's access requirements.

## How is the feed ordered?

The Recent view orders articles by recency. Other views, including Most upvoted and Best discussions, use different measures. Filters let you choose practice areas and jurisdictions for My feed.

## Can I use my own RSS reader?

Yes. Subscribe to [the public RSS feed](https://digest.legal/rss). It lists recent public articles. It does not include private content or your personal feed settings.

## How can I report a problem?

Use the report option on an article to report a problem with it. Use [Feedback](https://digest.legal/feedback) for other problems, questions, or publisher removal requests.
