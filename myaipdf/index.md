# myAIPDF

**A privacy-first, on-device PDF toolkit.**

myAIPDF lets you compress, split, merge, extract text from, scan, and translate
PDF documents — entirely on your device. Your files are **never** uploaded to
our servers or any third-party cloud. Your documents never leave your phone.

## Features

- **Compress** PDFs to reduce file size
- **Split & merge** documents
- **Extract text** and analyze content
- **Scan** documents with your camera
- **Translate** on-device using your operating system's built-in engine
  (Apple Translation on iOS 18+, Google ML Kit on Android) — no document text
  is ever sent to a server

## Availability

iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play).
